## Bottom line

OFFICIAL: 26/50. Submission Depth & Presentation: 3/10 (diagnostic, not folded in). Stage One: FAIL on gates 2, 3 and 4 — so as of today this entry is not eligible, and the 26 is academic until that changes. No previous review, so this is the baseline.

The panel is unusually unanimous, and unusually sympathetic. Five judges independently reached the same shape: this is a real, well-engineered, unusually self-honest build with no showroom. lib/youcam/ implements the genuine four-step Perfect Corp contract with signed presigned PUTs, Content-Length deliberately stripped, poll-to-task_status, 429/5xx retry, and semantic knowledge of the API most entrants never reach (SD/HD concern sets cannot be mixed; enable_mask_overlay for a presentable overlay; the lowest-health concern's mask chosen so the overlay matches the concern the plan names). The fused chain the tiebreaker rewards is architecturally real and I watched it run in 4.3s. The honesty machinery — /healthz's three-state model that refuses to probe because probes burn units, degradation to text instead of a fabricated render, demo-mode labelling in the first viewport, a README that volunteers its own past mis-gendering bug — is better than most winners ship.

And a judge gets none of it. No DNS, no deploy artifact, no published video, and origin/main is 8 commits behind without app/healthz, without HACKATHON.md, and without the cutPreference field — so the one judge who follows the README's own clone command lands on the un-mitigated mis-gendering behaviour and a 404 on the self-check chapter. That is the single weakest link: one failure to deliver, billed five times.

Three findings go beyond "unfinished" into things that must be fixed on principle, not for points. The refine flow makes a confirmed false tone claim, reproducible in two taps from a bundled preset, on the exact tap the video script films — integrity risk #3, materialized. An uploaded selfie in demo mode receives a stranger's scores and mask under the label "read from your own photo". And the provenance ledger tells the judge four Perfect Corp APIs ran in a run where none did. All three are small, local fixes by a builder whose instincts are already right — the demo flag reaches four surfaces and misses two; the tone check exists in the honest branch and is missing from the other. This is a seam problem, not a culture problem.

Order of operations is not negotiable: merge to main (five minutes, closes an integrity violation and the clone trap), fix the tone claim and the ledger, deploy, publish the video, submit Devpost, then spend units exactly once and commit the receipt. After that, favour the tiebreaker — the fusion-chain diagram and one units-on run on camera are what convert a chain the panel could only read into one it can watch. Also fix the date parser: a product whose entire premise is the plan for a specific day currently cannot read a specific day, and the Impact judge was right to lead with it.

Cut before you add. The three studio APIs, the makeup basket row, the zero-delta check-in, the dead lookVto, the stale screenshots and the older reel are all costing credibility they cannot repay.
